Privacy Policy

Namma Seniors · nammaseniors.com · Last updated: 5 November 2026

Namma Seniors is a student-built platform that connects students with senior mentors for 1:1 guidance. This page explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and profile picture when you sign in with Google, and your phone number when you enter it.
  • Booking details: the mentor you book, the topic or question you share, and the time of your session.
  • Payment details: payments are processed by Razorpay. We receive a payment reference and the payment status. We do not see or store your card, UPI or bank details.
  • Mentor applications: mentors share their name, college, branch, company or exam details, contact details, and a college ID for verification.

How we use your information

  • To log you in and keep your account secure.
  • To arrange sessions between students and mentors and send you the meeting link.
  • To process payments and mentor payouts.
  • To contact you about your bookings, for example on WhatsApp or email.
  • To verify mentors and keep the platform safe.

Google sign-in

When you use "Continue with Google", we only receive your name, email address and profile picture. We use them to create your account. We do not read your Gmail, contacts, files or any other Google data.

Who can see your information

We do not sell your personal information. A mentor you book can see your name and the question you submit. Our team can see booking and account details to run the service. Service providers such as Supabase (data storage), Razorpay (payments) and Vercel (website hosting) process data on our behalf.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active and as long as needed for bookings, payments and legal records. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information by writing to us using the contact below.
